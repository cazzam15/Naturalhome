## 07 — Recipe: Bicarbonate All-Purpose Spray

This one recipe will replace five things under your sink.

Four ingredients. Five minutes. Costs about 30p per bottle.

→ 250ml warm water
→ 1 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
→ 250ml white distilled vinegar
→ 10 drops tea tree or lemon essential oil

Swirl, fizz, combine. Works on kitchen worktops, bathrooms, glass, tiles — everything except natural stone and untreated wood.

## 10 — Recipe: Elderberry Immune Syrup

A batch costs about £2. It lasts the winter.

→ 100g dried elderberries
→ 600ml water
→ 2 cloves
→ 1 tsp fresh grated ginger
→ 3 tbsp raw honey (added off the heat)

Simmer for 20 minutes until reduced by half. Strain. Cool. Stir in honey. Store in the fridge for up to 8 weeks.

One tablespoon a day when you feel something coming on. No promises — but plenty of tradition behind it.

## 11 — Recipe: Winter Paw Balm

Salt, grit, and frozen pavements are hard on paw pads.

→ 2 tbsp beeswax pellets
→ 1 tbsp shea butter
→ 1 tbsp coconut oil
→ 1 tbsp calendula-infused oil

Melt together, pour into a small tin, cool. Apply a little to paw pads before walks in cold weather. Completely lick-safe.

Fifteen minutes, makes enough for a whole season.
