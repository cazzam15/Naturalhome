Recipe: Elderberry Immune Syrup
A batch costs about £2. It lasts the winter. → 100g dried elderberries → 600ml water → 2 cloves → 1 tsp fresh grated ginger → 3 tbsp raw honey (added off the heat) Simmer for 20 minutes until reduced by half. Strain. Cool. Stir in honey. Store in the fridge for up to 8 weeks. One tablespoon a day when you feel something coming on. No promises — but plenty of tradition behind it.
