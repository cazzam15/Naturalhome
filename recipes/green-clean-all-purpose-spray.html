Natural All-Purpose Cleaning Spray

Water, white vinegar and two essential oils. Two minutes to make and about 8p a bottle, against £2.50–£4.00 for the supermarket equivalent — the best money-per-minute swap in the book.

Time
2 mins
Makes
600 ml
Cost
~ 8p
Keeps
3 months

Ingredients

  • 500 ml water
  • 100 ml white vinegar
  • 10 drops lemon essential oil
  • 5 drops tea tree essential oil

You'll need

  • A glass spray bottle (or two, for 600 ml)
  • A microfibre cloth

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a spray bottle.
  2. Shake gently before each use.
  3. Spray onto surfaces and leave for 30 seconds before wiping with a microfibre cloth.
Safety

Do not use vinegar on natural stone (marble, granite, limestone, slate), waxed or oiled wood, cast iron or aluminium — and never mix it with bleach. Ventilate, and keep essential-oil sprays away from cats and other pets.

Why it works

The acetic acid dissolves greasy films and mineral marks; the oils add mild antimicrobial action and cover the short-lived vinegar smell.

Troubleshooting

  • Streaks on shiny surfaces mean too much vinegar or a dirty cloth — dilute further and use a fresh microfibre.
  • If the smell bothers you, swap plain vinegar for citrus-infused vinegar.

Keeps 3 months. There's no bicarbonate in this spray: bicarbonate and vinegar react once, fizz, and cancel each other out, so a stored mixture of the two is neither acid nor alkali.

For tougher jobs, try the overnight oven cleaner paste. This spray is the everyday workhorse.
