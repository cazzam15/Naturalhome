Overnight Oven Cleaner

Bicarbonate of soda, washing soda and water, spread thick and left overnight. Fifteen minutes of work, about 30p, and no shop-bought oven cleaner.

Time
15 mins + overnight
Makes
1 oven
Cost
~ 30p
Keeps
Make fresh

Ingredients

  • 200 g bicarbonate of soda
  • 3 tbsp washing soda (soda crystals)
  • Water, to form a thick, spreadable paste

You'll need

  • Rubber gloves
  • A damp cloth
  • Neat white vinegar in a spray bottle, for stubborn patches (optional)

Method

  1. Make sure the oven is completely cool and switched off at the mains. Remove the racks.
  2. Mix the powders with enough water to make a paste the consistency of thick porridge.
  3. Spread thickly over all interior surfaces, avoiding the heating elements and the fan.
  4. Leave overnight, eight hours minimum.
  5. Wipe out with a damp cloth, rinsing it frequently. For stubborn patches, spray neat white vinegar onto the residue, let it fizz for five minutes, and wipe again.
Safety

Washing soda is caustic and irritates skin and eyes: wear rubber gloves and avoid inhaling the powder. Keep it out of reach of children and pets. Don't use it on aluminium, or on waxed, oiled or lacquered surfaces. Switch off at the mains, and keep the paste off the fan and elements.

Why it works

Baked-on oven grime is polymerised fat — effectively varnish made of dinner. The strong alkalis slowly break the polymer bonds, which is why the overnight wait matters.

Troubleshooting

  • If the paste dried out before morning, mist it with water and give it two more hours.
  • If the grime won't shift, the layer was too thin — you shouldn't see the oven wall through it.

The glass door

Use a soft cloth or sponge on the glass, not a scourer. Which? warns that harsh scourers can leave tiny scratches that weaken the glass.

Which ovens?

Tested in an electric oven. If yours is gas, enamel-lined or self-cleaning (pyrolytic, catalytic or steam), check your oven manual before using any paste.

Green Tip

A weekly thirty-second wipe of the still-warm (not hot) oven with the all-purpose spray means you may never need the overnight treatment.

Make fresh each time. Don't pre-mix the vinegar into the paste: bicarbonate and vinegar react once, fizz, and cancel each other out. The vinegar goes on afterwards, only where you need it.
