The Author
01

A few jars, a kettle, and a kinder way of running a home.

The Natural Home Collection began with a question I couldn't shake — why are the products in our cupboards full of ingredients we'd never knowingly choose?

Most cleaning sprays, beauty products, and pet shampoos are built around the same handful of synthetic chemicals. They work, but they're hard on lungs, skin, waterways, and family budgets. The alternatives have been around for centuries: bicarbonate, vinegar, beeswax, lemon, plant oils, dried herbs.

What's been missing is a clear, modern guide to actually using them. So I wrote five — one for each room of the home where the difference matters most. Every recipe has been tested in a real UK kitchen, photographed, and sourced through ingredients you can buy on the high street.

This is not perfectionism. It's not a movement. It's just a calmer, less wasteful, slightly more beautiful way of doing the things you were going to do anyway.

— Caroline Morrison Author, The Natural Home Collection

The Method

Four quiet principles.

Plain ingredients

Bicarbonate, vinegar, beeswax, oats, oils. Every recipe in the collection is built from things that have been in our cupboards for centuries.

Slow rituals

Making your own takes a few minutes longer than buying. We think those minutes are the point — not a tax. The doing is the difference.

Honest about limits

Natural is not a synonym for safe or always-effective. We tell you when shop-bought is genuinely the better choice — and when it isn't.

Built for British homes

Metric measures, ingredients you can buy at any supermarket, and a tone that doesn't sound like it was translated from a Californian wellness blog.
